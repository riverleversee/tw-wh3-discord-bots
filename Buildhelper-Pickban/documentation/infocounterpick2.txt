This is a numerically optimized decision helper for counterpick in Pick 2 formats. \nIt takes your inputs as constraints (including opponent picks and bans), searches the matchup matrix, and returns all tied-optimal counterpick and ban combinations with expected matchup outcomes.\nThe avaliable options are= \npicked:\nbanned:\nunplayablefactions: \nopponentunplayablefactions: \nunbannable: \nglobalbans: \navoid: \nmustinclude:\nusematrix:\nDescription of options\npicked tells the command which 2 factions your opponent selected\nbanned tells the command which factions your opponent banned\nunplayablefactions tells the command what factions you do not want to play, or are unable to play because you already played them in the series \nopponentunplayablefactions tells the command what factions your opponent is unable to play because they already played them in the series. \nunbannable tells the command what factions you cannot ban because you already banned them or do not want to ban \nglobalbans tells the function what factionss are global banned and cannot be played by either player.\nmustinclude forces any given outputs to contain the factions in this list.\navoid removes any results from being printed that have you expecting to fight one of the listed factions (you may still see these factions, but it will be more favorable than those listed)\nThe options unplayablefactions, opponentunplayablefactions, mustinclude, and globalbans all take input in the same form. This is a string of faction acronyms seperated by a -. \nFor an input where you want to give Dwarfs, Dark Elves and Empire you would use the format: \n!pick2 [option name]: DW-DE-EM \nUse the command !factions to see the currently accepted faction acronyms\nThe option bannumber: tells the function how many bans you want to use in your pick 3 ban X, as many players prefer not to ban a faction in round 1, you may wish to set this to 0. The input to this function must be an integer.\nIf you have uploaded a matrix previously the command usematrix: [YOUR MATRIX NAME] allows for the results to come from the matrix you specified\n
