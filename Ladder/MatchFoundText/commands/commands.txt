There are a variety of commands in the bot for joining the Que, leaving it, reporting results, and updating preferences
1. Commands for joining, leaving, and checking the Que.
/join--Will add you to the Que. 
/leave--Will remove you from the Que. 
/queue_size--Will tell you the current number of players in Que. 
2. Commands for reporting results and contacting your opponent.
/report_result--Allows you to resolve a match, or exit. 
/send_message--Allows you to contact your opponent through a bot DM. 
/rules—shows you current unit bans/rules for your match
3. Updating your matchmaking preferences.
/update_mode--select your favorite game modes (Land/Dom)
/update_length--Select how long a series you want to play
/update_pick_system--Select how you like to do picks and bans 
/update_anonymous--Select if you want to have matches where the player identities are obscured. 
/update_global_bans--Select the type of global bans in your series. 
/update_unit_size--select the unit size you want to play with.
/view_params--View all of your current preferences. 
4. Check the leaderboard!
/leaderboard--Lists the top 10 elo players in the ladder.
